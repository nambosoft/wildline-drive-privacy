Wildline Drive — Privacy Policy

Last updated on 20 November 2026.

Who we are

Wildline Drive is an Android driving game developed by Nambosoft, based in Azerbaijan. Nambosoft is responsible for the game functionality described in this policy.

Privacy and support contact: nambosoft@gmail.com.

Scope

This policy describes the current game implementation. Wildline Drive is intended for players aged 13 and over and is planned for distribution through Google Play on Android.

Wildline Drive supports offline gameplay and stores progress on your device. The Android game also attempts to authenticate through Google Play Games when it starts. When authentication and an internet connection are available, it uses Google's Saved Games service to back up and restore game progress. Your Google Play Games settings determine the Google account used for this service.

Game information stored on your device

To operate the game and remember your progress, Wildline Drive stores:

  • Coin and diamond balances and nitro inventory.
  • Unlocked roads, selected vehicles and roads, and vehicle upgrade levels.
  • Distance records and game progress.
  • Daily task selections, progress, and reward claim status.
  • Dates and times used for daily rewards, free upgrade offers, and cooldowns.
  • Sound settings and other implemented preferences.

These records are used to provide gameplay, remember settings, calculate progress, and prevent repeated reward claims.

The game also stores a Google Play Games player identifier locally to associate cloud synchronization with the account first used on that installation, together with synchronization timestamps, checksums, and recovery information.

Google Play Games and cloud saves

When connected to Google Play Games, the game sends a progress snapshot to Google's Saved Games service. This snapshot includes coin and diamond balances, nitro inventory, unlocked vehicles and roads, upgrades, selected vehicles and roads, distance records, daily task data, and reward and upgrade offer history. This enables backup, synchronization, and restoration after reinstalling the game using the same Google Play Games account.

Offline progress is stored locally. Synchronization can resume when authentication and connectivity are available and the game is in the main menu. Where saved copies differ, the current implementation selects the progress with the later recorded modification time, based on the device clock. Unsynchronized progress may be lost if local app data is removed before a successful upload.

Google Play Games processes gamer identity, service analytics, and diagnostics as described in Google's documentation. Its SDK can collect analytics and diagnostic information automatically. Nambosoft uses the player identifier for cloud save association. Google's services handle authentication, and Nambosoft's game code does not request your Google password or Gmail address.

Google describes encryption in transit for Play Games Services data using HTTPS. Google's handling of personal information is described in its Privacy Policy. Further information about the SDK's data practices is available in Google Play Games data disclosure documentation.

Advertising and real-money purchases

The current version includes interfaces for rewarded features such as continuing after a crash, daily coin and diamond rewards, and free upgrades. Live advertising is currently inactive.

Live advertising and real-money billing are currently inactive. The shop in this version uses local test transactions.

Before live advertising or billing is enabled, this policy will be updated to identify the providers, data categories, purposes, sharing, retention information, and applicable privacy choices.

Contacting us

If you email Nambosoft, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to support or privacy requests and resolve the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support messages are handled through our email service provider and retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and meet applicable legal requirements. You may contact us to request deletion of your correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Sharing

Game progress is sent to Google for the cloud save functionality described above. Information you voluntarily send by email is processed through the email provider used to handle that correspondence. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Google Play and Android may independently process information when you download, update, or use their services. Their practices are described in Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

We will update this policy when additional advertising, billing, analytics, diagnostics, or other runtime services are added to Wildline Drive.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear the game's app storage, remove the game, or otherwise delete its local data. On Android, this is generally available under Settings → Apps → Wildline Drive → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data; names vary by device.

Clearing app storage removes your local progress, balances, unlocks, settings, and reward history. If a cloud save is available, signing in again can restore backed-up progress.

Cloud game data is retained through Google's Saved Games service until deleted through the available Google account and Play Games controls, subject to Google's retention practices. You can manage or delete data for Wildline Drive through your Play Games profile or the relevant Google account controls. Google's instructions are available at Delete your Play Games profile and data. Clearing or uninstalling the app alone does not delete its cloud save. To remove both local and cloud progress, close the game, remove its local data, and delete the game's Play Games data before opening the game again. Opening an installation with existing local progress can upload that progress again when synchronization is available.

Android or device backup and restore settings may retain or restore app data independently of the game's own functionality. Consult your device's backup settings if you also want to remove backups.

Google Play Games profile and account deletion are managed through Google's controls. For privacy requests about information handled by Nambosoft, including information you have sent by email, contact nambosoft@gmail.com.

Children and younger players

Wildline Drive is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not specifically directed to children under 13. Nambosoft does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 through the current game functionality.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact nambosoft@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

A 13+ target audience is not a statement that all players aged 13 can consent to every type of data processing in every country. Any consent and age-related requirements for future online services will be addressed before those services are enabled.

Security and privacy requests

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the information we handle. No storage or communication method is completely secure. Local preferences are gameplay storage and should not be treated as secure storage for sensitive information.

Depending on your location and the information involved, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy choices. Contact nambosoft@gmail.com with your request. We may request only information reasonably necessary to understand and verify the request.
